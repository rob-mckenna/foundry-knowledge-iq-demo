# Coverage Criteria — Medical Necessity
## BluePeak Health Plan — Clinical Policy CP-2026-MED-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 | **Review Date:** January 1, 2027
**Policy Owner:** Medical Management & Clinical Affairs
**Applies To:** All BluePeak commercial and Medicare Advantage plans unless otherwise noted

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines medical necessity criteria for selected procedures and services. BluePeak Health Plan uses evidence-based clinical criteria to determine whether a requested service is medically necessary. These criteria are based on nationally recognized clinical guidelines including those from:

- American College of Cardiology / American Heart Association (ACC/AHA)
- American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS)
- American Diabetes Association (ADA)
- U.S. Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF)
- Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) — MAPD

A service is **medically necessary** when it is:
1. Clinically appropriate for the member's condition
2. In accordance with generally accepted standards of care
3. Not primarily for the convenience of the provider or member
4. Not experimental, investigational, or unproven

## 2. Cardiovascular Services

### 2.1 Echocardiography — Transthoracic (CPT 93306, 93307, 93308)

**Covered Indications (at least ONE must be met):**
- New or changed cardiac murmur with hemodynamic significance
- Evaluation of known or suspected structural heart disease (e.g., valvular abnormality, cardiomyopathy, congenital defect)
- Symptoms consistent with heart failure (dyspnea, orthopnea, edema) with clinical signs
- Atrial fibrillation or other arrhythmia requiring assessment of left ventricular function or structural disease
- Stroke or TIA with suspected cardioembolic source
- Syncope with suspected cardiac etiology
- Preoperative evaluation for high-risk cardiac surgery
- Follow-up for known cardiomyopathy or EF monitoring (no more frequently than every 6 months without change in clinical status; every 12 months if stable)

**Not Covered (examples):**
- Routine screening in asymptomatic patients without cardiac history
- Repeat study within 6 months without documented change in clinical status
- Echocardiography solely for clearance of non-cardiac surgery in low-risk patients

---

### 2.2 Cardiac Stress Testing (CPT 93015–93018)

**Covered Indications:**
- Evaluation of known or suspected coronary artery disease (CAD) in patients with symptoms (chest pain, dyspnea, fatigue) — standard treadmill preferred when EKG interpretable and patient able to exercise
- Risk stratification post-myocardial infarction (≥ 4 days post-MI)
- Preoperative evaluation for intermediate or high-risk non-cardiac surgery in patients with ≥ 3 cardiac risk factors
- Evaluation of exercise-induced arrhythmia

**Pharmacologic Stress (CPT 93015 + 78452 or 93351):**
Indicated when patient unable to exercise adequately (e.g., significant peripheral artery disease, musculoskeletal limitations, LBBB on resting EKG).

**Not Covered:**
- Routine annual cardiac clearance without symptoms or cardiac history
- Repeat stress test within 2 years of normal study without change in symptoms
- Pre-participation sports screening in patients < 40 without cardiac symptoms

## 3. Musculoskeletal Services

### 3.1 Advanced Imaging — Musculoskeletal MRI (CPT 73221–73223, 73721–73723)

**Covered Indications (at least ONE must be met):**
- Suspicion of internal derangement (e.g., meniscal tear, ACL/PCL rupture, labral pathology) where findings would change management
- Evaluation of soft tissue mass of uncertain etiology
- Avascular necrosis evaluation
- Occult fracture not visible on plain film in a patient with high clinical suspicion
- Evaluation of infection (osteomyelitis, septic joint) when X-ray inconclusive
- Pre-surgical planning for complex joint replacement or reconstruction

**Step Requirements — Knee MRI:**
- Plain radiographs (weight-bearing AP, lateral, and Merchant views) must be obtained first unless acute trauma
- Conservative management (minimum 6 weeks) required for chronic knee pain unless acute injury

**Not Covered:**
- MRI of asymptomatic contralateral extremity without clinical indication
- Repeat MRI within 6 months of prior study without documented change in condition
- MRI solely to screen for pathology in absence of symptoms

---

### 3.2 Physical Therapy (PT) — Extended Course (> 20 visits per year)

**Covered when ALL criteria are met:**
1. Active treatment goal documented (not maintenance therapy)
2. Measurable functional progress demonstrated with validated outcome tool (e.g., LEFS, NDI, PSFS) over prior authorized period
3. Treating therapist attestation that skilled PT is required (cannot be performed by unskilled caregiver or through home exercise program)
4. PA required for visits 21+ per benefit year (see PA Policy CP-2026-PA-001)

**Maintenance Therapy:** Physical therapy that maintains current level of function without expectation of improvement is **not covered** under medical benefit. Members may access wellness programs at applicable plan rates.

## 4. Mental Health and Substance Use Disorder Services

### 4.1 Inpatient Behavioral Health

BluePeak covers inpatient psychiatric admissions when:
- Member presents with an acute psychiatric crisis (active suicidal ideation with plan, psychotic break, acute mania)
- Outpatient or intensive outpatient treatment has been tried and failed, or is clinically inappropriate given severity
- Treatment requires 24-hour nursing supervision and psychiatric management
- Admission is to a BluePeak in-network behavioral health facility (out-of-network requires emergency circumstances or prior approval)

**Continued Stay Criteria (reviewed every 3–7 days):**
- Continued imminent risk that cannot be managed in less restrictive setting
- Active participation in treatment plan
- Progress toward discharge goals documented

---

### 4.2 Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) Therapy

**Covered Indications:**
- Member has a diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Level 1–3, by a licensed psychologist or psychiatrist
- Treatment plan developed and supervised by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA)
- PA required for all ABA services; re-authorization every 6 months with updated behavioral assessment

## 5. Preventive Services — Coverage Without Cost Sharing

The following preventive services are covered at $0 cost sharing for in-network providers per ACA Section 2713 and applicable USPSTF Grade A/B recommendations:

| Service | Recommendation | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Blood Pressure Screening | USPSTF Grade A | Annually (adults 18+) |
| Colorectal Cancer Screening (colonoscopy) | USPSTF Grade A | Every 10 years (ages 45–75); every 5 years if high-risk |
| Colorectal Cancer Screening (stool-based: FIT, Cologuard) | USPSTF Grade A | Annually (FIT); every 1–3 years (Cologuard) |
| Mammography | USPSTF Grade B | Every 2 years (ages 40–74) |
| Diabetes Screening | USPSTF Grade B | Every 3 years (BMI ≥ 25, ages 35–70) |
| Lung Cancer Screening (LDCT) | USPSTF Grade B | Annually (ages 50–80; 20 pack-year history; current/recent smoker) |
| Statin Therapy (preventive) | USPSTF Grade B | As indicated (ages 40–75; CVD risk ≥ 10%; 1+ risk factors) |
| Depression Screening | USPSTF Grade B | Annually (adults 18+) |

*MAPD members: preventive services also follow CMS guidelines; some services may have additional Medicare coverage criteria.*

## 6. Experimental and Investigational Services

Services that are experimental, investigational, or unproven are **not covered** under any BluePeak plan. A service is considered experimental/investigational when:
- It is the subject of ongoing Phase I, II, or III clinical trials as the intervention arm
- It is not approved by the FDA for the requested indication
- There is insufficient peer-reviewed clinical evidence establishing its safety and efficacy

**Exception — Clinical Trial Coverage:** BluePeak covers routine patient costs associated with participation in qualifying clinical trials for life-threatening conditions per ACA Section 2709 and CMS requirements for MAPD.

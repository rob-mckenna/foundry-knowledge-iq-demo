**Policy Owner:** Medical Management & Clinical Affairs  
**Applies To:** All HealthBridge commercial plans and Medicare Advantage (MAPD) unless otherwise noted

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines prior authorization (PA) requirements for advanced diagnostic imaging, including Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), Computed Tomography (CT), Positron Emission Tomography (PET), and Nuclear Medicine studies. Advanced imaging carries significant cost and may expose members to radiation; PA ensures clinical appropriateness and appropriate utilization.

PA applies to **outpatient and elective** advanced imaging. Emergency imaging ordered for life-threatening conditions does not require advance PA but may require concurrent notification.

## 3. Clinical Criteria — MRI Lumbar Spine (CPT 72148)

HealthBridge will approve lumbar spine MRI when **at least one** of the following criteria is met:

**Covered Indications:**
1. Back pain with neurological deficit (radiculopathy, loss of bowel/bladder control, foot drop)
2. Low back pain refractory to **at least 6 weeks** of conservative management including:
   - Trial of analgesics (NSAIDs unless contraindicated)
   - Physical therapy or home exercise program
3. Suspected epidural abscess, spinal tumor, or osteomyelitis (any of: fever, unexplained weight loss, history of cancer, IV drug use, night pain unrelieved by rest)
4. Post-surgical evaluation (hardware complications, infection, failed back surgery syndrome)
5. New neurogenic claudication consistent with spinal stenosis not resolved with conservative measures

**Imaging Not Approved (Exclusions):**
- Non-specific low back pain of < 6 weeks duration without red flags
- Routine follow-up imaging when prior study was normal and no clinical change
- Imaging for administrative/disability purposes only (requires separate review)

## 4. Clinical Criteria — MRI Brain (CPT 70553/70552/70551)

HealthBridge will approve brain MRI when **at least one** criterion is met:

1. New or worsening headaches not responsive to therapy, or headaches with concerning features (worst headache of life, thunderclap onset, positional component, associated neurological symptoms)
2. New onset seizure disorder
3. Suspected demyelinating disease (e.g., multiple sclerosis)
4. Cognitive decline with clinical evaluation warranting structural CNS assessment
5. Monitoring of known intracranial lesion, vascular malformation, or post-treatment oncology follow-up
6. Stroke/TIA workup
7. Vertigo refractory to 4 weeks of conservative management

## 5. Submission Requirements

All PA requests must include:
1. Member ID, date of birth, and plan ID
2. Ordering provider NPI, specialty, and contact
3. Specific CPT code(s) and body region
4. ICD-10-CM diagnosis code(s)
5. Clinical notes supporting criteria (H&P, failed conservative management documentation, specialist evaluation)
6. Proposed imaging facility (in-network preferred)

**Submission Channels:**

## 7. Alternative Imaging Guidance

HealthBridge follows **ACR Appropriateness Criteria** as the evidence base for imaging PA decisions. Providers are encouraged to consult ACR guidance before ordering advanced imaging to ensure clinical appropriateness. Orders from radiologists confirming appropriateness criteria are weighted favorably in clinical review.

---

## 8. Denial and Appeals

If imaging PA is denied:
- Written denial with specific clinical reason provided within applicable timeframe
- Peer-to-peer review available within 72 hours of denial for urgent cases
- First-level appeal: reviewed by radiologist or relevant specialist within 30 days (standard) / 72 hours (urgent)
- External IRO review available for experimental/medical necessity denials

# Prior Authorization Policy
## BluePeak Health Plan — Clinical Policy CP-2026-PA-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 | **Review Date:** January 1, 2027
**Policy Owner:** Medical Management & Clinical Affairs
**Applies To:** All BluePeak commercial and Medicare Advantage plans unless otherwise noted

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Prior Authorization (PA) is a utilization management program requiring advance approval from BluePeak Health Plan before certain services are rendered. This policy defines the services requiring PA, the submission process, review criteria, and timelines applicable to BluePeak network and out-of-network providers.

PA does **not** imply that a service is medically necessary, nor does it guarantee payment. Final claim adjudication is subject to eligibility, benefit design, and applicable exclusions.

Requests may be submitted via:
- **Provider Portal:** bluepeak-provider.example.com → "Auth Request"
- **Fax:** 555-0199 (secure)
- **Phone:** 555-0190 (Provider Services) — for urgent requests only

All requests must include:
1. Member ID and date of birth
2. Requesting provider NPI and contact information
3. Procedure / service code(s) with description
4. Diagnosis code(s) — ICD-10-CM
5. Clinical notes supporting medical necessity (H&P, imaging reports, specialist letter)
6. Expected service date and facility (if applicable)

Timeframes reflect regulatory requirements under Illinois Insurance Code 215 ILCS 5/155.22a and CMS Medicare Advantage guidelines.

## 4. Medical Necessity Criteria — Joint Replacement

### 4.1 Total Knee Arthroplasty (CPT 27447)

BluePeak will approve Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA) when **ALL** of the following criteria are met:

**Clinical Criteria:**
1. Diagnosis of severe knee osteoarthritis (ICD-10: M17.11, M17.12, M17.31, M17.32) confirmed by X-ray (weight-bearing AP, lateral, and Merchant views) showing joint space narrowing, osteophyte formation, or subchondral sclerosis
2. Significant functional impairment: pain rated ≥ 7/10 on VAS with activities of daily living, or documented inability to perform two or more ADLs
3. Failure of **at least 3 months** of conservative management, documented in medical record, including:
   - NSAIDs or other analgesics (unless contraindicated)
   - Physical therapy (minimum 6 sessions)
   - At least one intra-articular injection (corticosteroid or hyaluronic acid) — unless contraindicated
4. BMI ≤ 40 (cases with BMI > 40 require bariatric consultation or additional documentation)
5. Medically fit for surgery (clearance documentation from PCP or relevant specialist)

**Not Covered (Exclusions):**
- Isolated patellofemoral arthritis without tibiofemoral involvement
- Revision TKA within 2 years of prior TKA without documented mechanical failure or infection
- Unicompartmental knee replacement in patients with bi- or tri-compartmental disease

## 5. Denial and Appeals

If a PA request is denied, BluePeak will provide:
- Written denial notice within the applicable timeframe
- Specific clinical reason for denial
- Information on the appeals process

**Appeal Levels:**
1. **First-level appeal:** Reviewed by a clinician of the same specialty. Decision within 30 days (standard) or 72 hours (urgent).
2. **Second-level appeal:** Peer-to-peer review or Independent Review Organization (IRO) referral.
3. **External review:** Available under Illinois law for experimental or medical necessity denials.

---

## 6. Policy Exceptions

Providers may request a clinical exception for services not meeting criteria if:
- The member has a unique clinical circumstance not addressed by criteria
- Published clinical literature supports the requested approach
- The treating physician attests that the standard criteria create an undue risk

Exceptions are reviewed by the BluePeak Medical Director and are not guaranteed.

# Specialty Pharmacy and Biologic Drug Prior Authorization Policy
## HealthBridge Insurance Plan — Clinical Policy CP-2026-PA-004
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 | **Review Date:** January 1, 2027  
**Policy Owner:** Pharmacy Management & Clinical Affairs  
**Applies To:** All HealthBridge commercial plans and Medicare Advantage (MAPD)

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs prior authorization (PA) requirements for specialty medications (Formulary Tier 4 — Specialty Preferred and Tier 5 — Specialty Non-Preferred), including biologics, immunomodulators, oncology agents, and other high-cost specialty drugs. PA ensures these medications are used for covered, evidence-based indications, that step therapy requirements have been met, and that appropriate dispensing through preferred specialty pharmacies occurs.

All specialty medications require PA. There are no specialty drugs that are automatically covered without advance authorization.

---

## 2. Step Therapy Requirements

Step therapy (also called "fail first") requires members to try one or more preferred, lower-cost medications before a specialty drug will be approved. Step therapy requirements are condition-specific.

### 2.1 Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA) — Biologics

**Applicable medications:** adalimumab (Humira), etanercept (Enbrel), abatacept (Orencia), tocilizumab (Actemra), biosimilars (Hadlima, Cyltezo, Hyrimoz)

**Required Step Therapy:**
1. **Step 1:** Trial of at least **two** conventional DMARDs at maximally tolerated doses for a minimum of **3 months each**:
   - Methotrexate (preferred first DMARD)
   - Hydroxychloroquine (Plaquenil)
   - Sulfasalazine
   - Leflunomide (Arava)
2. **Step 2 (preferred):** If Step 1 fails — trial of a **biosimilar** adalimumab (e.g., Hadlima, Cyltezo) is preferred over originator Humira
3. **Step 3:** Originator adalimumab (Humira) approved only if biosimilar is contraindicated, not tolerated, or member has documented clinical reason for branded preference

**Approval Criteria (RA):**
- Diagnosis of moderate-to-severe RA confirmed by rheumatologist (ICD-10: M05, M06)
- Documentation of prior DMARD trial(s) with dates, doses, and reason for discontinuation
- Disease activity score (DAS28 or CDAI) demonstrating inadequate control
- Negative TB screening (IGRA or PPD) within 12 months of initiation

---

### 2.2 Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) — Biologics

**Applicable medications:** adalimumab (Humira), infliximab (Remicade), vedolizumab (Entyvio), ustekinumab (Stelara)

**Required Step Therapy (Crohn's Disease / Ulcerative Colitis):**
1. **Step 1:** Adequate trial of conventional therapy (mesalamine for UC; immunomodulators — azathioprine, 6-MP, or methotrexate for Crohn's) for minimum 3 months
2. **Step 2:** Corticosteroid-dependent or corticosteroid-refractory disease documented
3. **Biologics approved** when Step 1–2 criteria are met and GI specialist confirms moderate-to-severe disease (endoscopic or radiographic evidence)

---

### 2.3 Oncology Agents

**Applicable medications:** pembrolizumab (Keytruda), nivolumab (Opdivo), trastuzumab (Herceptin), bevacizumab (Avastin), and other antineoplastic agents

**Step therapy does not apply to oncology.** Approval is based on:
1. Histologically confirmed malignancy with documented staging
2. Drug is indicated for the specific tumor type and line of therapy (first-line, second-line, etc.) per NCCN guidelines
3. Prescribing oncologist attests that regimen is consistent with evidence-based guidelines
4. For immunotherapy: PDL1 testing or MSI/MMR status documented when guideline-required

---

### 2.4 Diabetes — GLP-1 and SGLT-2 Agents

**Applicable medications:** semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy), liraglutide (Victoza), dulaglutide (Trulicity), empagliflozin (Jardiance), dapagliflozin (Farxiga)

**Note:** GLP-1 and SGLT-2 agents are Tier 3 non-specialty (not subject to specialty PA), but require step therapy when used for weight management (Wegovy):

**Weight Management Step Therapy:**
1. BMI ≥ 30 kg/m² (or ≥ 27 with obesity-related comorbidity) documented
2. Minimum 3-month structured behavioral intervention on record
3. Trial of at least one first-line pharmacotherapy (orlistat, phentermine) unless contraindicated

## 3. PA Clinical Documentation Requirements

All specialty PA requests must include:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Member ID & plan | Required for all requests |
| Prescribing provider NPI | Specialty attestation for biologics |
| Drug name, dose, frequency | Exact NDC or J-code preferred |
| Diagnosis (ICD-10-CM) | Must match indication |
| Step therapy documentation | Dates, drugs trialed, doses, outcome |
| Lab/test results | TB test, disease activity scores, biomarker testing |
| Specialist note | Confirming diagnosis and plan |

---

## 4. Preferred Specialty Pharmacy

HealthBridge members must obtain specialty medications through a **preferred specialty pharmacy**:

- **HealthBridge Specialty Pharmacy (Preferred):** 1-800-555-SPEC | HealthBridge-specialty.example.com
- **CVS Specialty (Preferred):** Available for select specialty categories
- **Non-preferred specialty pharmacies:** Covered at 50% of allowed amount; member responsible for cost difference

Specialty medications dispensed through a non-preferred pharmacy without HealthBridge authorization will be denied unless a hardship or access exception has been pre-approved.

---

## 5. Quantity Limits

Quantity limits are applied per FDA-approved dosing and clinical guidelines. Quantities exceeding approved limits require a **quantity limit exception** submitted concurrently with or after the initial PA.

---

## 6. Authorization Duration

| Drug Class | Standard Authorization Period |
|---|---|
| Biologics (RA, IBD, psoriasis) | 12 months (initial); 12 months (renewal with documented response) |
| Oncology agents | Per treatment cycle per regimen; reassessed each cycle |
| GLP-1 (weight management) | 6 months (initial); 6 months (renewal with documented ≥ 5% weight loss response) |
| Immunosuppressants (transplant) | 12 months with annual review |

---

## 7. Denial and Appeals

Specialty drug denials are subject to the same appeal process as medical PA denials:
1. **First-level appeal:** Reviewed by a clinical pharmacist or physician of appropriate specialty within 30 days
2. **Peer-to-peer:** Available by calling 555-0190 within 5 business days of denial
3. **External review:** Available for medical necessity denials under Illinois law

**Clinical Exception:** If step therapy cannot be completed due to a contraindication, prior documented failure outside the HealthBridge plan, or urgent clinical need, providers may request a **step therapy exception** with supporting documentation.

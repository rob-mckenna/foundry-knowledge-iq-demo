## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs prior authorization (PA) requirements for outpatient Physical Therapy (PT), Occupational Therapy (OT), and Speech-Language Pathology (SLP/Speech Therapy) services. HealthBridge supports medically necessary rehabilitation services and uses utilization management to ensure services are goal-directed, time-limited, and resulting in functional improvement.

## 4. Clinical Criteria for Continued Therapy Authorization (Visits 21+)

HealthBridge will approve continuation of PT/OT/SLP beyond 20 visits when **ALL** of the following are met:

1. **Active treatment plan** on file from a licensed therapist with measurable, functional goals and target completion date
2. **Documented functional improvement** from baseline using a validated outcome measure (FOTO, LEFS, NDI, Oswestry, etc.):
   - Minimum clinically important difference (MCID) threshold must be trending toward achievement
3. **Reasonable expectation** of continued progress — services must be directed toward a specific functional goal (not maintenance only)
4. **Appropriate setting** — outpatient services are appropriate and the member does not require a higher level of care (home health, inpatient rehab)
5. **Attending physician or specialist concurs** with continued therapy (note within the last 60 days)

**Maintenance Therapy Exclusion:** Services aimed solely at maintaining current function (maintenance therapy) are not covered unless the member has a condition requiring skilled therapy to prevent regression (e.g., progressive neurological disease). Standard maintenance programs are excluded.

---

## 5. Clinical Criteria — Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)

All ABA services require PA regardless of visit count. HealthBridge follows BACB (Behavior Analyst Certification Board) standards. PA criteria:

1. Member has a documented diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) per DSM-5 criteria (ICD-10: F84.0)
2. Treatment plan developed by a Board-Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) with measurable behavioral goals
3. Initial authorization: up to 10 hours/week for 90 days, with reassessment
4. Continued authorization: documented progress toward goal reduction targets; BCBA reassessment every 90 days

## 6. Submission Requirements

**Initial PA Request (prior to visit 21):**
Submit no later than visit 18 to avoid a gap in coverage. Required documentation:
1. Member ID and plan information
2. Referring/requesting provider NPI
3. Treating therapist NPI and credentials
4. Diagnosis codes (ICD-10-CM) supporting medical necessity
5. Initial evaluation report including baseline functional status
6. Treatment plan with specific goals, frequency, duration, and expected outcomes
7. Session notes for visits 1–20 (progress summary acceptable)

**Continued Authorization Requests:**
- Submit 7–10 days before current authorization expires
- Include interval treatment notes showing objective functional improvement
- Validated outcome measure scores (baseline and current)

**Submission Channels:**
- Provider Portal: HealthBridge-provider.example.com → "Rehab Services Auth"
- Fax: 555-0198

## 8. Denial and Appeals

Denials for continued therapy are reviewable:
- **First-level appeal:** Reviewed by a Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation (PM&R) physician or relevant specialty clinician within 30 days (standard) or 72 hours (urgent/concurrent)
- **Peer-to-peer:** Available within 5 business days of denial — call 555-0190
- **External review:** Available under Illinois law for medical necessity denials

## HealthBridge Insurance Plan — Clinical Policy CP-2026-FRM-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 | **Review Date:** January 1, 2027
**Policy Owner:** Pharmacy & Therapeutics Committee
**Applies To:** HealthBridge commercial plans and Medicare Advantage (MAPD); exceptions noted

## 1. Overview

The HealthBridge Formulary is a list of covered prescription drugs organized into cost-sharing tiers. The Pharmacy & Therapeutics (P&T) Committee reviews and updates the formulary quarterly. Members and providers can access the complete drug list at **HealthBridge-rx.example.com**.

This document summarizes formulary tier structure, step therapy requirements, quantity limits, and specialty drug criteria.

## 3. Step Therapy Requirements

Step therapy requires members to try one or more preferred medications before a higher-tier or specialty drug is approved. The following therapeutic categories have mandatory step therapy protocols:

### 3.1 Hypertension (First-Line Antihypertensives)

**Required Step 1 (Tier 1 Generics — try at least ONE for minimum 30 days):**
- Lisinopril (ACE Inhibitor)
- Amlodipine (Calcium Channel Blocker)
- Hydrochlorothiazide / Chlorthalidone (Thiazide diuretic)
- Losartan (ARB)

**Step 2 (Tier 2 — if Step 1 inadequate or contraindicated):**
- Metoprolol succinate (Beta-blocker)
- Olmesartan (ARB — if intolerant of losartan)
- Valsartan/Hydrochlorothiazide (combination)

**Step 3 (Tier 3 — Brand, requires PA + step documentation):**
- Sacubitril/Valsartan (Entresto) — approved for HFrEF; hypertension use requires PA

---

### 3.2 Diabetes — Type 2

**Required Step 1 (Tier 1):**
- Metformin (immediate or extended release)
- *Exception: Metformin contraindicated in eGFR < 30; step waived with documentation*

**Required Step 2 (Tier 1–2 — at least ONE for 90 days before proceeding):**
- Glipizide or Glimepiride (sulfonylurea)
- Sitagliptin (DPP-4 inhibitor — Tier 2)

**Specialty Access (Tier 4–5 — requires step documentation + PA):**
- GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (e.g., semaglutide, liraglutide, dulaglutide): Requires failure of Tier 1 AND at least one Tier 2 agent; HbA1c ≥ 7.5 documented within 6 months; prescriber attestation of cardiovascular or renal comorbidity (for MAPD: aligns with CMS coverage determination)
- SGLT-2 Inhibitors (e.g., empagliflozin, dapagliflozin): Requires failure of metformin; PA required for Tier 4

---

### 3.3 Inflammatory Conditions (Biologics / JAK Inhibitors)

**Covered Conditions:** Rheumatoid Arthritis, Psoriatic Arthritis, Ankylosing Spondylitis, Plaque Psoriasis, Crohn's Disease, Ulcerative Colitis

**Step Therapy Protocol:**

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Step 1 (≥ 3 months) | Conventional DMARD: Methotrexate AND/OR Hydroxychloroquine (RA/PsA); Aminosalicylate (IBD) |
| Step 2 (≥ 3 months) | Preferred Biologic: Etanercept (Enbrel) or Adalimumab biosimilar (preferred biosimilar list — not originator) |
| Step 3 (Specialty PA) | Non-preferred biologics: Ustekinumab, Secukinumab, Ixekizumab, Risankizumab |
| Step 3 Alt (Specialty PA) | JAK Inhibitors: Tofacitinib, Baricitinib, Upadacitinib — requires failure of biologic AND black box warning counseling documented |

**Step Waiver Criteria:**
- Prior documented failure (adverse event, intolerance, or inadequate response) satisfies step
- Step waiver request submitted via PA portal with supporting documentation

## 4. Quantity Limits

Quantity limits (QL) apply to specific drugs to align with FDA-approved dosing and reduce waste.

| Drug / Class | Quantity Limit |
|---|---|
| Opioid analgesics (acute) | 7-day supply per fill (initial prescription); 30-day supply for chronic pain with PA |
| Triptans (migraine) | 9 units per 30 days |
| Erectile Dysfunction agents | 8 units per 30 days |
| GLP-1 agonists (e.g., semaglutide) | 1 pen/injection device per 28 days; 4 per 90 days via mail |
| Ophthalmic drops | As labeled; 1 bottle per 28 days per eye |
| Inhaled corticosteroids | 1 inhaler per 30 days (2 for short-acting rescue) |

## 5. Non-Formulary Drugs and Exceptions

### 5.1 Non-Formulary Drugs

Drugs not on the HealthBridge Formulary are generally **not covered** except via exception. A formulary exception may be approved when:
1. All formulary alternatives in the therapeutic class are contraindicated
2. The member has tried and failed all covered alternatives
3. A covered alternative would cause adverse effects based on documented clinical history

### 5.2 How to Request a Formulary Exception

1. Prescriber submits PA request via HealthBridge-rx.example.com/pa-request
2. Include: member ID, drug name/dose, NDC code, diagnosis, step therapy documentation, clinical rationale
3. Decision within 72 hours (standard) or 24 hours (urgent)

## 6. Specialty Pharmacy

All Tier 4 and Tier 5 medications must be dispensed through **HealthBridge Preferred Specialty Pharmacy network**. Members who obtain specialty drugs at a retail pharmacy will incur a non-preferred penalty unless an exception is granted.

**Preferred Specialty Pharmacy Partners:**
- CenterRx Specialty (mail — nationwide)
- MedPoint Specialty Pharmacy (retail — IL locations)
- Integrated Specialty Solutions (mail and retail)

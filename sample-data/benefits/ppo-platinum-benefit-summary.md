## Out-of-Network Coverage

This is a **PPO plan.** You have the freedom to see any provider. However:
- In-network providers offer the highest level of benefits and lowest cost sharing
- Out-of-network services carry a higher deductible and coinsurance
- Inpatient rehab and SNF (days 21+) are not covered out-of-network
- **Balance billing:** Out-of-network providers may bill you for the difference between their charge and HealthBridge's allowed amount

**No Surprises Act:** For emergency services and certain facility-based care, you will not be balance-billed beyond in-network cost sharing.

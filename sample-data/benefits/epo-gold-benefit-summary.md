## Out-of-Network Coverage

This is an **EPO (Exclusive Provider Organization) plan.** Unlike a PPO:
- Services outside the HealthBridge EPO network are **not covered**, except for true medical emergencies
- There is **no out-of-network benefit** for elective or scheduled care
- Members who use out-of-network providers for non-emergency services will be responsible for the full cost

**No Surprises Act:** For emergency services and certain facility-based care, you will not pay more than in-network cost sharing regardless of provider network status.

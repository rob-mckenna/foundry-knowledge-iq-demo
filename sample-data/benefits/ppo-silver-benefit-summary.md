## Out-of-Network Coverage

This is a **PPO plan.** You have the freedom to see any provider. However:
- In-network providers offer the highest level of benefits and lower out-of-pocket costs
- Out-of-network services are covered but subject to the out-of-network deductible and higher coinsurance
- HealthBridge does not cover out-of-network preventive care or inpatient rehab/SNF at out-of-network facilities
- **Balance billing:** Out-of-network providers may bill you for amounts above HealthBridge's allowed amount

**No Surprises Act:** For emergency services and certain facility-based care, you will not be balance-billed beyond in-network cost sharing regardless of provider network status.
